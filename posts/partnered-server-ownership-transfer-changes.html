Changes to Partnered Server Transfers

Discord has adopted new policies in regards to ownership of Partnered servers. This change was communicated via a partner-only channel on Discord Admins.

Effective immediately all partnerd servers will lose their status upon ownership transfer, or in the event the owner is compromised without possibility of recovery.

  • When transferring ownership, you must confirm you're okay with the perks being stripped from both yourself and the server. The new owner inherits nothing.
  • Compromised partner accounts without the possibility of recovery are disabled, and the account and all partnered servers owned by them are stripped of its status.

This change is introduced in an effort to make accounts far less desirable targets for bad actors. Partnership dies with you.

"While we do not comment on Trust & Safety procedure as a general rule, I felt it was important to share these changes with you as part of an effort to make your accounts less attractive targets for compromise and takeover. We will continue to review hacked account reports on their individual merits. As always, take measures to safeguard your account from bad actors who might seek to steal your credentials or compromise your system."
