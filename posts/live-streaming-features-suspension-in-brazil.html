Streaming features suspension in Brazil

Discord has restricted Go Live features for users in Brazil to comply with a court order after the death of a 13-year-old girl. Users in the affected region will be unable to screenshare or enable their cameras, and will consequently also be unable to view any livestreams or video of others.
