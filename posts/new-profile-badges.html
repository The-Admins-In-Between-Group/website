Quality of Life  July 31, 2026

New Profile Badges

Discord has introduced five new "milestone" profile badges.

  • Account Age: how long you've been on Discord.
  • Game Variety: how many different games you've played.
  • Game Time: how long you've spent playing games.
  • Streaming: how long you've spent streaming on Discord.
  • Gifting: how many gifts you've purchased on Discord (both Nitro and Cosmetics count towards the progression and you can also gift yourself).

The badges are retroactive, though as Discord hasn't always been tracking these stats, dozens if not hundreds of your games players and hours may not be accounted for.

Read all about the new badges (and the numbers required for each milestone) in the A list of Discord badges - Library post.
