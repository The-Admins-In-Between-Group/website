Active Developer Badge Discontinuation

Discord has decommissioned the Active Developer Badge that was previously ontainable by running a Slash [/] command on your owned bot. The badge is removed from all the profiles that had it. The alleged reason behind it was the "heavy amount of support issues with little benfit to developers". Read about it in detail here.
