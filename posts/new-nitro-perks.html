Nitro  May 11, 2026

New Nitro Perks

↺ - Updated article. Review the changes under the article.

Over the coming weeks, Nitro members will start seeing 3 brand new perks for their subscription: Xbox Game Pass, gaming gear discounts, and bonus Orbs.

The price of Nitro will NOT be increasing, staying at $9.99.

Xbox Game Pass

Users with an active Nitro subscription get Xbox Game Pass with their subscription at no additional cost. The subscription is for the Starter Edition and includes:

  • 50+ games like Stardew Valley, DayZ, and Fallout 4
  • 10 hours of cloud gaming each month
  • Play and earn up to $25 a year in the Store with Rewards

This partnership works both ways, eligible Xbox Game Pass subscribers will soon also receive Discord Nitro benefits starting later this month.

Gaming Gear Discounts

Users with an active Nitro subscription now automatically build up to discounts for Logitech G, SteelSeries and KontrolFreak. A discount can be claimed every month.

  • Up to -30% at Logitech G
  • Up to -15% at SteelSeries
  • Up to -15% at KontrolFreak (No longer available)

More Orbs

Users with an active Nitro subscription will now receive 250 Orbs every month with their subscription, as well as earn a bonus on top the base amount for completing Quests.

↺ Update (June 12, 2026): KontrolFreak discount no longer available

As of July 1, 2026, the KontrolFreak discount is no longer available. New brand discounts are expected to enter rotation at a later time.

↺ Update (Oct 14, 2026): New Best Buy perk

As of October 14, 2026, Nitro subscribers can claim one year of My Best Buy Plus (worth $29.99/year) for free. Reedemable by Nov 18, 2026.
