A foreword.

Discord has shifted its priorities, and as a result many of its community programs no longer exist, or do so in limbo — as is the case with Discord Admins. It was a great place for networking with like-minded people, thanks to a diverse assortment of users from every corner of Discord, from small servers to multi-million member communities.

Unfortunately, the bar for entry has been set to an unrealistic level with no changes in sight: no shortcuts, no exceptions, and an uncertain future overhead.

We want to bring that knowledge here. For those not in Discord Admins, the next best option is Town Hall, where many nuanced and complex questions go unanswered — questions we likely already have an answer to, and the freedom to discuss.

The Admins Inbetween is a community made by admins, for admins, with a goal of bridging that gap.

Not affiliated with Discord  ·  A companion project to killedbydiscord.com
